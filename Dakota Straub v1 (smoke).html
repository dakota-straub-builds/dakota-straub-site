I'm Dakota Straub — an independent designer working with founders and small teams on the bits of their brand that show up everywhere: identity, websites, packaging. Before going solo I spent five years inside studios in New York and Berlin.

I take on a small number of projects each quarter so I can do the work myself, end to end. No account managers, no decks-about-decks, no surprise invoices.
